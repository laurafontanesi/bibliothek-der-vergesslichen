# The data

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `words.json` | the words, one entry each (fields below) |
| `grammar.json` | the grammar curriculum and the sentences of the grammar topics |
| `schema.json` | the lists Python and the website share: levels, word types, kinds of related word, topics with their definitions |
| `karten.json` | the word and collocation cards; generated by `python -m vocab_builder karten` |
| `grammar_cards.json` | the grammar cards; generated by `python -m vocab_builder grammar cards` |

Do not edit the two generated files by hand. The data is under CC BY-SA 4.0 ([LICENSE-DATA.md](../../LICENSE-DATA.md)).

## words.json

Every entry has:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `id` | the entry's identifier, used in the addresses of the website (`der-aufzug`) |
| `word` | the German word in its canonical form, nouns with their article |
| `type` | `noun`, `verb`, `adj/adv`, `prep/conj`, `expression`, `construction` or `other` |
| `definitions` | `[{meaning, note}]`, the meanings in English, the first one being the main meaning |
| `definition_de` | a German definition of the first meaning (German Wiktionary, DWDS, or written by hand); optional |
| `examples` | `[{de, en}]`, example sentences with their translation |
| `notes` | a short note, or null; register goes here when it is not neutral (`Register: umgangssprachlich.`) |
| `topics` | 0 to 2 topics from the closed list in `schema.json`; empty for core vocabulary |
| `related` | `[{word, kind}]`, related words in `words.json`, always in both directions; the kinds are in `schema.json` (`related_kinds`). Verb families are not stored here but by `family_root` |
| `collocations` | `[{de, en, note?, example?}]`, phrases with the word, one card each (English to German) |
| `compound_parts` | for compounds: the parts in order, linking elements as their own item (`["die Kündigung", "-s-", "die Frist"]`); optional |
| `level` | `A1`, `A2` or `B1` when the word is in that Goethe-Zertifikat word list, else `B2+`; `level_manual: true` keeps a level set by hand |
| `dwds` | `{url, freq}`: the DWDS article and its frequency class, 0 (rare) to 6 (very frequent); `manual: true` keeps a link set by hand |
| `added` | the date the word was added (YYYY-MM-DD) |
| `exercises` | verbs only: the gap-fill sentences of the verb cards (below) |

Extra fields by word type:

| Type | Field | Description |
|---|---|---|
| noun | `gender`, `plural` | `der`, `die` or `das`; the plural with its article |
| noun | `plural_card` | `false` when the plural should not be asked on the Produzieren card |
| verb | `auxiliary` | `haben` or `sein` |
| verb | `past_tense`, `present_3sg` | Präteritum and the er/sie/es form of the present, main-clause form, with `sich` for reflexive verbs (`nahm mit`, `freut sich`) |
| verb | `past_participle` | without `sich` |
| verb | `imperative`, `konjunktiv_2` | the du-imperative only when the stem changes (`nimm mit`); the one-word Konjunktiv II only where it is in everyday use (`käme`) |
| verb | `verb_class` | `regular`, `irregular` or `mixed` |
| verb | `is_separable`, `prefix`, `family_root` | separable or not, the prefix (`mit-`), the root verb (`nehmen`) |
| verb | `reflexive` | `"akk"`, `"dat"` or `false`: reflexive means the verb cannot drop *sich*, or changes its meaning with it |
| noun, verb, adj/adv | `preposition` | the fixed preposition with its case (`an + DAT`); several separated by ` / ` |
| adj/adv, prep/conj | `usage` | `both`, or which of the two uses the word has |
| adj/adv | `derived_from` | the verb of a participle adjective (`entspannen` for *entspannt*) |

`exercises` are `[{slot, label, de, en, blanks, source}]`. `slot` is `praet`, `part`, `praes` or `refl`; `blanks` are character spans in `de` (`[[4, 8]]` hides *nahm* in "Ich nahm ein Taxi…"); `verb_en` is the English verb shown in the hint. `source` is `own`, `example`, `tatoeba:<sentence id>` or `generated` (written with AI and passed by the sentence checker).

## grammar.json

- `structures`: the curriculum, one entry per grammar structure from A1 to C1, with an id `<topic>-<nn>`, its `level` (and `goethe_level` where the Goethe-Institut places it differently), its `thread`, the card type it needs (`card`: `gap`, `choice`, `build`, `transform`, or `word card` when the word cards practise it) and its `status` (`active`, `planned`, `word-cards`, `writing`, `reading`, `later`). The names of the structures stay private.
- `threads`: structures that come back at several levels share a thread and one refresher.
- `topics`: what the grammar cards are built from. A topic covers some structures and is divided into cells; each cell holds sentences with `de`, `en`, `blanks` (as in `exercises`), `word_id` (the word it practises, if any), `source`, `status` (from the checker) and `use` (on the cards, or kept in reserve). `en_hl` marks by hand the part of the English to highlight, in braces; `cue` replaces the automatic cue.
- `sources`: what the curriculum is based on.

## Sources

- **Morphy / LanguageTool full-form lexicon** (CC BY-SA 4.0, [danielnaber.de/morphologie](https://danielnaber.de/morphologie/)): checking verb and noun forms, filling the forms of new words, the conjugation tables on the cards.
- **German Wiktionary** (CC BY-SA, via [kaikki.org](https://kaikki.org/dewiktionary/)): German definitions and the parts of compounds.
- **DWDS lemma list** ([dwds.de](https://www.dwds.de/d/api)): the frequency class and article link of each word, looked up offline. Source: „Lemmadatenbank", in: DWDS – Digitales Wörterbuch der deutschen Sprache, hrsg. v. d. Berlin-Brandenburgischen Akademie der Wissenschaften, heruntergeladen am 30.10.2026.
- **Goethe-Zertifikat word lists A1 to B1** (copyright Goethe-Institut, downloaded from DWDS): only the `level` of each word; the lists themselves are not published.
- **Tatoeba** (CC BY 2.0 FR, [tatoeba.org](https://tatoeba.org/en/downloads)): some example and exercise sentences. They keep their sentence id (`tatoeba:699936`, and `en_source` when the English is from Tatoeba too), and the card shows the credit under the sentence.
- **AI** (Claude, Anthropic): many English meanings, notes, example and exercise sentences and collocations.
- The grammar curriculum is based on grammar checklists and grammar books used as references only (`sources` in `grammar.json`); its ids, grouping and order are this project's own.
